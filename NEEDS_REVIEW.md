## Game-design choices that are *not* Highway Code rules
- **Fault severities.** The game decides which mistakes are minor, serious or dangerous. These mirror the spirit of DVSA marking but are simplified. DVSA examiners judge each fault in context. An ADI should review the table in `src/rules/faults.ts`.
- **"Mirror check within 4 seconds before signalling/moving off."** The Highway Code says "in good time". 4 s is a game tuning value.
- **Signalling to move off in an empty car park.** Rule 159 says "signal if necessary". The game does not fault a missing signal when no one would benefit, but the guided drive teaches it as a habit. Check this framing.
- **Car park speed limit 10 mph.** Private car parks set their own limits; 10 mph is a typical posted limit, not law.
- **DSSSM cockpit drill (Doors, Seat, Steering, Seatbelt, Mirrors).** This is a widely taught instructor mnemonic, not Highway Code wording. Rule 97 covers the substance.
- **Pass mark 80% and star thresholds** are game rules.
- **Week 1 fault severities.** Rolling over a STOP line = serious. Pulling out with another car under ~2.2 s away = serious, under 1 s = dangerous. Passing parked cars on your side with oncoming traffic under 35 m = minor, under 15 m = serious. Passing a parked car with under 0.7 m gap = minor. Crossing a solid double white line = serious. Entering a No Entry road = serious. Pulling up on double yellows, within 10 m of a junction or on double whites = serious; single yellow = minor (we assume the restriction is in force); more than 1.2 m from the kerb = minor. An ADI should sanity-check these against DVSA marking guidance.
- **Turn checks.** A turn is a heading change of more than ~70°. The signal must be on when the turn starts; a mirror check must be within 8 s of the turn, or within 8 s before the signal went on (MSM on the approach). Road bends (Tannery Road/Back Lane) are exempt.
- **Junction look-check.** "Look right and left" means using both shoulder-look controls within 8 s before crossing the give way/stop line.
- **Progress fault.** Below 40% of the limit for 10 s on a clear road (not near junctions, parked cars or a requested stop). DVSA marks undue hesitation and progress, but there is no single Highway Code rule for it, so the fault cites none.
- **Moving-off blind spot.** Required when moving off from a parked position (kerb or handbrake), not when pulling away in a traffic queue.
- **Sign artwork.** Our signs are original drawings following the shapes and colours described in the Highway Code. Please check visually: the national speed limit sign (diagonal from top right to bottom left), the 20 zone entry and end signs, the "50 yds" distance plate under the give way warning, and the No Waiting disc.
- **Town geometry.** Lanes are 3.8 m wide with 5 m kerb radii at junctions. Real roads vary.
- **AI traffic.** AI cars give way to oncoming traffic before passing parked cars on their side and queue behind stopped vehicles. They never run red lights or behave badly, which real traffic sometimes does.

- **Week 2 severities.** Joining a roundabout in front of circulating traffic: under ~2.2 s serious, under 1 s dangerous. Wrong approach signal, no left signal when leaving, wrong lane: minor. Going round the wrong way: dangerous. Driving over a mini-roundabout’s centre: serious. Red light (or red and amber): serious. Going on at amber with plenty of room to stop: minor (judged from where you were and your speed when it changed; 3 m/s² braking plus 4 m). Passing someone on a crossing: serious, or dangerous if they were close to your path; someone waiting at a zebra or a junction mouth: minor. Stopping on a crossing: minor; stopping in a box junction (not turning right): serious; driving in a bus lane for 2 s: minor; pulling up on zig-zags or School Keep Clear markings: serious. Passing a cyclist under 1.4 m (1.9 m above 30 mph): serious, under 0.7 m: dangerous; a horse under 1.9 m or above 11 mph: serious (small allowances for sim precision). Not looking all round before reversing: minor. Finishing over a bay’s lines or more than ~13° crooked: minor; parallel park more than 0.5 m from the kerb: minor.
- **Timed boss (Rush Hour).** A 3-minute countdown is shown; running out of time before the finish is a serious fault. This is a game rule, not a Highway Code one; a careful scripted drive takes about 2 minutes in busy traffic. The briefing tells players never to rush a junction to beat the clock.
- **Roundabout signals.** Exits are classed by angle: under 135° = left, over 225° = right, otherwise ahead. Signals on minis are judged on the approach only (there’s little time to signal off). The indicator doesn’t self-cancel while you are on a roundabout.
- **Signal timings.** Junction green 14 s, amber 3 s, all-red 2 s, red and amber 2 s. Pelican: 8 s minimum green, red 7 s, flashing amber 6 s. Puffin: red held 6–16 s while people are on the crossing. Real timings vary by site.
- **Pedestrians.** People arrive at crossings at random. At a zebra (and a junction mouth) they step out once traffic has stopped or is more than ~4.5 s away. A pedestrian counts as “waiting” after 1.2 s at the kerb, so drivers aren’t faulted for someone who appears at the last moment.
- **The Magic Roundabout.** Ours is a simplified ring of three mini-roundabouts round an island, inspired by real multiple-roundabout junctions. It is not a copy of any real site.
- **Bus lane hours.** Canal Side’s bus lane is “at any time”. Timed bus lanes aren’t modelled yet (the game has no clock).
- **Manoeuvres.** Bays are 2.5 × 4.8 m and the parallel gap is about 9.6 m (2.3 car lengths), a little generous for a game. Reverse parking is judged at the end (inside the lines, angle). Rule 201’s “reverse in, drive out” is taught, not enforced.
- **Cyclists and horse riders** ride steadily near the kerb and never wobble or turn. The passing check measures the closest side-to-side gap while you are alongside.

- **Week 3 severities (in `FAST_TUNING`).** Following under 2 s (4 s in the wet) for 3 s: minor; under 0.9 s: serious. Lane change without a mirror check in the last 6 s, or without a signal: minor. Crossing a slip road’s solid line: minor; joining into a gap under 1.6 s of the car behind: serious; joining below 40 mph: minor. Sitting in lane 2 or 3 for 15 s with lane 1 clear: minor (lane hogging). Undertaking a moving vehicle: minor. Hard shoulder for 2 s, a red-X lane for 0.5 s, reversing on a motorway: serious. The wrong way down a carriageway: dangerous. Stopping on a motorway for 3 s: serious. More than 5 mph over a bend’s advisory speed: minor. No headlights in the dark or in fog for 4 s: serious; main beam dazzling someone for 2.5 s: minor.
- **Week 3 world.** Lanes and motorway lanes are 3.65 m; the single-track lane is 3.6 m with 28 m passing places. Slip roads, tapers and corners are simplified rectangles. Variable limits are fixed per gantry (60 then 50), not reacting to traffic. Lorries cruise at about three-quarters of the limit; AI keeps about a 1.9 s gap and never tailgates or undertakes.
- **Wet grip.** Braking grip is 60% in the wet; the emergency-stop report doubles the typical figure in the rain, as the Highway Code says stopping distances “at least double”.
